Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email vibelolabs@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened just before the problem.

Questions

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide. Tap the scooter button when it is charged.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is saved only on the device where you play. There is no account or cloud save yet, and deleting the app deletes your progress.

The game feels slow or stutters. What can I try?

Close other apps, turn off Low Power Mode and restart the game. If it continues, let us know your iPhone model.

An ad did not give me a reward.

A reward is given only when the video has played to the end. If the video finished and you still got nothing, restart the game and email us what happened.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

For tracking, open iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. If your region requires an ad consent form, a Privacy options button appears on the main menu.

How do I turn off gameplay statistics?

Use the Anonymous stats button on the main menu.
