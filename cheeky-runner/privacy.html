Cheeky Runner

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Cheeky Runner has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number. We have no game server of our own. The game uses Mixpanel to help us improve it and Google AdMob to show ads, and this page explains what each one does.

Who we are

Cheeky Runner is published by Mert Karabacak (Vibelolabs). For any privacy question, write to vibelolabs@gmail.com.

Saved on your device

Your coins, stars, upgrades, level, game statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see this file or restore it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Gameplay statistics (Mixpanel)

We use Mixpanel to learn how the game is played, for example where players get stuck. Mixpanel stores this data for us in the European Union.

When statistics are on, the game sends:

  • Game activity: app opens and session length, screens viewed, buttons tapped, runs started and finished, stage, score, distance, coins earned, upgrades, level, and whether an ad loaded or was completed.
  • A random ID: a number created on your device so that events from the same install can be grouped together. It is not your name, your email or Apple's advertising identifier.
  • Technical details: app version, operating system, device type and screen size.

We instruct Mixpanel not to use your IP address to determine your location. We do not record your screen or create a personal profile of you.

Your choice: use the Anonymous stats button on the main menu to turn statistics on or off at any time. Where consent is required, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, statistics stay off unless you turn them on.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional videos that you can choose to watch for a reward. Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad views, ad interactions and app diagnostics.

Google uses this data to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. It acts under its own terms, described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Your iPhone may ask whether the game can track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Where required, including the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice. You can change it with the Privacy options button on the main menu.

Sharing and sale of data

We share data only with Mixpanel and Google as described above, and when the law requires it. We do not sell your data for money. Ads that use identifiers may count as "sharing" for advertising under some US state privacy laws, and the tracking choice above is how you can limit it.

How long data is kept

We keep gameplay statistics only as long as they are useful for improving the game, and then delete them. Google keeps ad data according to its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK and US state privacy laws may give you the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority.

We do not hold your name or contact details, so we usually cannot connect statistics to a particular person. You can stop future collection with the controls above, or write to us and we will help however we can. Requests about ad data can also be made to Google.

Children

Cheeky Runner is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
